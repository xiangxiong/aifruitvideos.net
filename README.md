## 💡 Original Prompt Ideas to Try

Use these as starting points for your own experiments. They are suggested prompts, not verified output examples.

### 🍑 Peach Morning Greeting

```text
A tiny peach character stands beside a white teacup and waves one little hand toward the camera. Warm morning sunlight, soft fuzzy peach texture, cheerful expression, uncluttered kitchen background. One continuous close-up, centered portrait composition.
```

### 🍐 Crystal Pear Slice

```text
A translucent emerald glass pear rests on a matte white cutting board. A knife makes one slow slice through the pear, revealing delicate crystal layers. Macro camera, controlled studio reflections, clean background, one continuous shot. Request a soft glass-like slicing sound.
```

### 🍍 Pineapple Dance Break

```text
A small cartoon pineapple wearing round sunglasses performs a gentle side-to-side dance on a pastel yellow stage. Full character visible, expressive face, soft studio lighting, simple background, fixed camera, centered vertical composition.
```

### 🍋 Lemon's Big Surprise

```text
A tiny lemon character opens a miniature gift box and discovers an oversized blueberry inside. The lemon pauses, then makes one exaggerated surprised expression. Bright tabletop scene, playful animation, clear silhouettes, one continuous shot, no text or logos.
```

**Prompt tip:** Describe the fruit, one action, the camera, and the lighting. Keep the scene simple enough to read in six seconds, then change one detail at a time as you experiment.
